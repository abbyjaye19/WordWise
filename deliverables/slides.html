WordWise closes the gap between who needs reading support and who receives it, at near-zero marginal cost

Situation

About 5–6% of U.S. students are formally identified with a reading-based learning disability, and up to 15–20% of people show some symptoms of dyslexia.

The most effective help, specialized instruction, is expensive and hard to find.

Complication

Most struggling readers never qualify for formal support. They piece together separate tools (text-to-speech, dictionaries, general chatbots) that don't share context, ignore reading level, and can replace the reading instead of building comprehension.

Resolution

WordWise: one workspace on the student's own PDFs, slides, and documents, with read-aloud and display controls, plus four focused AI agents that explain text beside the original at one of four reader levels and check understanding with questions.

Four single-call agents sit behind one guard layer, so cost and failure modes stay bounded

Exhibit 4 · System architecture

1

AI only where it adds value. Display, read-aloud, highlights, and folders cost $0.

2

Single-call, tool-less agents. One click = one call; no loops, so cost and failure modes are bounded.

3

One guard layer on every call: budget cap, input caps, <reading> delimiting, model check, logging.

4

One shared, cached prefix (safety rules + reader-level guide) across agents.

5

Student data stays local: accounts and saved work never reach the model.

Source: app/services/ai.py, prompts/; per-call costs are measured warm-cache averages (output/tokenomics/summary.json).

Why caching worked

Prompts of ~650 tokens sat below the provider's 1,024-token caching minimum (0 cached tokens on every call). A shared ~1,600-token prefix now has 89% of input billed at the cached rate (10× cheaper).

Scale implication

The $2/day default cap protects a demo; at 10,000 typical users spend is ~$5.50/day, so the cap must become per-student.

Source: measured with scripts/measure_tokenomics.py; gpt-6-luna prices per 1M tokens: input $0.10, cached $0.01, cache write $0.125, output $0.50. Usage levels (4 / 12 / 30 sessions a month) are assumptions.
