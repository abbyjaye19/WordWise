How Bees Make Honey

Honeybees collect nectar from flowers. They carry it back to the hive in a special stomach called the honey stomach.

Inside the hive, worker bees pass the nectar from bee to bee. Each time, the nectar loses a little water. The bees store it in wax cells and fan it with their wings until it becomes thick honey.
